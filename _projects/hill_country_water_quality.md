From 2023 to 2024 I established 20+ surface water monitoring sites at the UT Hill Country Field Station. The goal of the project was to characterize the baseline water quality of the area, and assess hydrochemical facies and groundwater flow paths. I led monthly sampling campaigns and processed samples for geochemical analyses. Later, I passed off the project to an incoming graduate student, and focused on the dendrochronology of the same region while remaining involved in data analysis and interpretation.
